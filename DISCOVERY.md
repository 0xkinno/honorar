## 2. Observed constraint

Source: Circle docs, "Batched settlement" (read 2026-10-14). A buyer signs an EIP-3009 authorization off chain. Gateway locks the buyer funds and credits the seller pending balance. Periodically Gateway computes net balance changes across participants inside a TEE and applies them in one on chain transaction. The individual payment does not appear on chain.

## 4. Why generic approaches do not solve it

Showing a payment as settled when the payment call returns reports an authorization, not a settlement. Trusting model citations pays sources that were never quoted.

## 5. New capability enabled

Every AI read and every AI quote becomes individually auditable on Arc: verified quotes become Merkle leaves committed with the payment, and reading fees are tracked to their batch transaction.
