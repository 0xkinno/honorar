## 2. Observed constraint

Source: Circle docs, "Batched settlement". A buyer signs an EIP-3009 authorization off chain. Gateway locks the buyer funds and credits the seller pending balance. Periodically Gateway nets balance changes inside a TEE and applies them in one on-chain transaction.

## 4. Why generic approaches do not solve it

Reporting a payment as settled when the payment call returns reports an authorization. In E1 the same authorization took between 53.4s and 875.1s to settle. Trusting model citations pays sources that were never quoted.

## 5. New capability enabled

Every AI read and every AI quote becomes individually auditable on Arc: verified quotes become Merkle leaves committed with the payment, and each reading fee is tracked to the batch transaction that settled it.

## 6. Invariant

I2 USE: no span is paid unless it appears quoted in the answer.

## 7. Failure mode

B3 citation stuffing, and B8 seller drop after signing. Results in `evidence/break/`.
